Use Stack / Migrated / Model TWT, or enable Flip-flop, to compare each section with the original model converted to vertical two-way time. On the unmigrated stack flat reflectors approximately align but dipping events do not: their reflection point is not the surface midpoint. Run Migrate stack and compare again — diffractions collapse, dipping reflectors move updip toward their true position, and a syncline bow-tie unties.
Teaching assumptions

2D variable-density acoustic simulation; simple hyperbolic NMO. Available processing: top mute, zero-phase bandpass, semblance and constant-velocity-stack velocity analysis, Dix inversion, NMO, CMP stack and post-stack Kirchhoff time migration.

Still absent: elastic modes (no shear or ground roll), statics, DMO, pre-stack migration, residual moveout, anisotropy correction and deconvolution. Migration here is post-stack and uses the stacking velocities, so it inherits any error in them; its amplitude treatment applies obliquity only, without the rho filter or geometric spreading, so positions are meaningful and relative amplitudes are only indicative.

CMP stacking alone does not correct dip — that is what the migration step is there to show. DMO would still be needed where conflicting dips share a midpoint.
